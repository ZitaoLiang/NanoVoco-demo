Upsampling-Aware Rank–Width Reallocation

Complete TTS on a microcontroller

The paper integrates Flite 2.2, EfficientSpeech, and NanoVoco on an Arduino Portenta H7 (Cortex-M7, 480 MHz), using A16W8 neural models and only on-chip memory.

  • 0.74 s median speech startup delay from on-device text processing.
  • 0.9876 median corpus compute real-time factor; no reported I2S underflows across 128 texts × 5 passes.
  • 1,823,784 B Flash and 991,616 B data SRAM for the complete system; no external SDRAM.

The complete TTS pipeline scores 3.299 UTMOS in FP32 and 3.227 with A16W8. These are separate end-to-end results from the vocoder-only comparison above. This release provides the floating-point vocoder; the displayed samples are vocoder comparisons.
